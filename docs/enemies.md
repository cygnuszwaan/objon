# Enemies

Drillbot, Stingbot, Grunt, Tread crawler, Proximity mine and Rail bot (with its sparks). Read `objon.md` (the core: setup, layers and groups, contracts, invariants, docs index) first.

**Group `"enemy"`** is what the zone sensor (`power.md`) counts when `count_enemies` is on, so membership matters: every enemy joins it in its own `_ready()`; a Grunt, Tread crawler, Proximity mine and Rail bot leave it as they start to die, while a Drillbot and a Stingbot stay in it until `queue_free()` takes them at the end of that frame (the sensor also skips a body that is queued for deletion). A Spark is not an enemy and is never counted.

**Enemy weight.** Every enemy except the rail bot has an authored `load_weight` export (group "Load") and answers `get_load_weight() -> float`: its `load_weight` while it is alive and `is_on_floor()`, else 0. The weight plate and the see-saw read it through group `"enemy"` and `has_method()`, in the units of a block's `mass` (metal 4, ice 1 per tile, the player 2), scaled by their own `enemy_weight_scale` (plate default 1, see-saw default 0); both also count what stands on a weighing enemy, like a block. Defaults: Grunt 2, Drillbot 2, Tread crawler 2 (a flipped one too: it stays on the floor), Proximity mine 1, Stingbot 1 (only while landed: it counts during its landing and recharge, never in flight). The rail bot hangs overhead and has no weight. A dying enemy weighs nothing from the frame it starts to die. Weight is not saved: it is an authored value that is only read.

Covers: Drillbot; Stingbot; Grunt (States; Ledge and wall tests; Hammer; Placeholder art; Checkpoint); Tread crawler (Flipping; Surf; Checkpoint; Placeholder art; Sounds); Proximity mine (Blast; Kick; Checkpoint; Placeholder art; Sounds); Rail bot (Motion; Sparks; Power contract; Damage; Checkpoint; Placeholder art; Sounds)

- **Drillbot** (`drillbot.gd`): patrols, `CharacterBody2D` body is solid/harmless/kickable; a separate front-only `DrillHazard` `Area2D` is the only hazardous part, polled the same way as the sawwheel/crusher. Facing mirrors the body sprite, `DrillHazard`'s position, and its (duplicated per-instance) polygon collider points all together around local x=0. It implements the surf contract `bowled_over(direction, surf_speed) -> bool`: true (and `_die()`) only when the surfer is travelling the way the drillbot faces, i.e. coming up behind it; from the drill side it returns false, so the surfer meets `DrillHazard`, `take_hit()` ends the surf and the drill wins. **Free patrol**: with `patrol_distance` at 0 (it is `@export_range(0, 512, 1, or_greater)`; the default stays 96) it has no set range and patrols like the Tread crawler: after each move, a wall ahead of the **drill's tip** (`_wall_ahead()`: a `test_move()` of the body forward by `_drill_overhang`, how far the drill hazard's front edge sticks out past the body collider's, read from the two shapes in `_ready()`, plus `WALL_PROBE_MARGIN` 1 px; a hit counts only if it opposes the facing and is steeper than `floor_max_angle`, so a walkable slope isn't a wall; `is_on_wall()` stays as a backstop; the body collider alone would turn it only once the drill was already buried) or a ledge ahead (`_ledge_ahead()`, the Grunt's and the crawler's `test_move()` probe: the body shifted forward by its own width + 1 and dropped `LEDGE_DROP` 12 px; the width is read from the body shape, the polygon's x extent) starts the usual dwell, then it turns round. `_origin_x` is unused in that mode. A bot restored from a checkpoint at a wall or ledge simply dwells again.
- **Stingbot** (`stingbot.gd`): flying `CharacterBody2D` (mask narrowed to layer 2, same reasoning as Drillbot's mask) that homes toward the player. A single `enum Phase` (`AIRBORNE` → `LANDING` → `RECHARGING` → `LAUNCHING` → back to `AIRBORNE`) drives the flight/recharge cycle: `flight_time` (exported) counts down while airborne before it lands (straight descent to `is_on_floor()`), then `recharge_time` (exported) holds it grounded before a fixed-duration launch back into the air. While airborne it recomputes, every frame, which diagonal side of the player it's currently on and seeks a hover spot on that side; once there, it jabs with a short lunge at the player and then recoils straight away for a fixed duration — the recoil **is** the attack's cooldown/grace window, rather than a separate timer, since it's what puts distance between it and the player before the next approach. Damage is two `Area2D`s (`StingerLeft`/`StingerRight`) polled every physics frame the same way as Drillbot's `DrillHazard` — no explicit "is attacking" gate needed, since proximity from the geometry alone is what makes them overlap the player during a jab. Health/death/hit-feedback (3 hits, flash+shake, death explosion, damage sound) are copied verbatim from Drillbot. No obstacle avoidance yet — it seeks the hover position in a straight line and can get stuck against a wall between it and that spot.
- **Grunt** (`grunt.gd` + `grunt_art.gd`, `grunt.tscn`; behaviour built, art is **placeholder rectangles**): a skinny android with a hammer. `@tool` `CharacterBody2D` (6x16 rectangle collider, origin at its centre, `collision_layer` 1, `collision_mask` 2 like Drillbot) in groups `"enemy"` and `"saveable"`. `health` (default 2 kicks) and `facing` (`Left:-1`/`Right:1`) are **authored starting values only**: runtime state is `_health` and `_facing`, so a play-test never writes an export. `floor_snap_length` is set to 6 px at load so it walks down slopes without hopping. Implements the three contracts the rest of the game uses: `take_damage(amount) -> int` (honours `amount`, unlike Drillbot's; the last kick uses the standard explosion, a non-lethal one flashes the art), the surf contract `bowled_over(direction, surf_speed) -> bool` (always true, from either side and in every state: the art is thrown away spinning and fades over 0.7s, the body, hammer and groups are retired at once so a checkpoint taken mid-tumble excludes it, then it frees itself), and `save_state()`/`load_state()`. The body is solid (the player stands on it, `KickHitbox` sees it); whether a bowling surfer should pass through it is decided by the bowl sweep, which excludes it with a collision exception. Sounds `enemies/grunt_hit`, `enemies/grunt_bowled`, `enemies/grunt_windup` and `enemies/grunt_swing` are in `Audio.PLANNED`. Unlike Drillbot and Stingbot it has no shared base with them; a small `Enemy` base is still an option, not done.
  - **States** (`enum State`, decided each physics frame from the world; `_elapsed`/`_duration` time the current one): `PAUSE` (still, hammer upright, random time in `pause_time_min..max`; on ending it rolls `turn_chance` to turn round, then walks), `WALK` (`wander_speed`, random time in `walk_time_min..max`; a wall in its heading or a ledge ahead turns it round into a PAUSE whose end does not roll the random turn), `CHASE` (`chase_speed`, faces the player each frame; at a ledge it stands still instead of walking off, at a wall the move stops it; there is no turning and no pathing), `WINDUP` (`windup_time`, facing locked, the telegraph), `SWING` (`swing_time`, the only live state), `RECOVER` (`recover_time`, hammer down and inert, the grunt open). Aggro: the player within `aggro_radius` horizontally and `aggro_height` vertically (a dead or dancing player never counts) takes PAUSE/WALK to CHASE; CHASE lets go beyond `lose_radius` (larger, so the edge doesn't flicker it) and returns to PAUSE. CHASE enters WINDUP at `attack_range` (default 14 px centre to centre; vertical limit half of `aggro_height`). Once WINDUP begins the three attack states run to the end regardless of the player; RECOVER goes back to CHASE if the player is still within `aggro_radius`, else PAUSE. The player is looked up lazily from group `"player"` (assigned in `player.gd`'s `_ready()`).
  - **Ledge and wall tests**: `_ledge_ahead()` is `test_move()` from the body shifted `LEDGE_PROBE_SHIFT` (7 px: its width plus 1) forward, going down `LEDGE_DROP` (12 px): no hit means a ledge, so it turns with the centre about 4 px from the edge. `test_move()` honours one-way catwalks and counts a slope or wall ahead as floor, so a 45° slope (7 px drop) is not a ledge and only a real drop is. The wall test is `is_on_wall()` with the wall normal opposing the heading, after the move, so slopes within the floor angle are not walls.
  - **Hammer**: a separate `Area2D` (`HammerPivot/Hammer`, layer 0, mask 1, monitoring off) under a `HammerPivot` `Node2D` at the shoulder (0, -4); it only holds the hitbox, a 4×16 rectangle starting at the pivot. Hits come from `_sweep_hammer()`, **not** from the Area's overlaps: an overlap is a physics step stale, and the swing eases in so most of its angle is covered in its last frames, so polling the Area never saw the hammer past about 40° (only a player pressed against the grunt was hit, and a crouching or surfing one ducked it). The sweep is an immediate `intersect_shape()` of the hammer rectangle (mask 1, the grunt excluded) at every 6° (`HAMMER_SWEEP_STEP_DEGREES`) along the arc travelled since the last frame, ending on the pose about to be drawn; the frame that ends the swing still sweeps to `swing_angle_degrees`. It calls `take_hit(damage, global_position)` on any body that has it, **only on frames that began in SWING**, once per swing, counted only when `take_hit()` returns true (a hit eaten by i-frames can still land later in the swing). Held upright (angle 0) when idle, walking, chasing and at the end of recovery. The angle (degrees from upright, positive forward) comes from the state and its phase each frame: WINDUP eases from 0 to `windup_angle_degrees` (-70, back over the shoulder; fast lift, then holds), SWING eases in from there to `swing_angle_degrees` (115, forward and down), RECOVER holds it down for the first half and then eases back to 0. The pivot is not mirrored by scale (a negative scale on a collider is unreliable): `HammerPivot.rotation` is the angle times `_facing`. Against the player's colliders the swept hammer first overlaps at roughly 63-76° into the downswing (over the head) and reaches a player 4-18 px in front when standing, 4-17 px when crouched (a crouching or surfing player does not duck it: the downswing ends below shoulder height); it is harmless through the whole windup, and a player standing within about 14 px *behind* the grunt can be hit at the start of the swing, when the hammer passes back through upright. There is no speed rule and no immunity: a surfing player bowls the grunt during the windup, before a swing can land, so the telegraph settles the bowl/hit ordering. A kick does not interrupt a windup.
  - **Placeholder art** (`grunt_art.gd`): plain rectangles drawn in code, facing right and mirrored by `scale.x` = `_facing`. The body rectangle and the hammer rectangle are read from the parent's collision shapes, so what is drawn is the hitbox; a small visor marks the facing side. The hammer's colour is its state: grey harmless, amber windup, red live swing. `Grunt` hands it the angle and mode through `set_pose(angle, mode)`; replace it with an `AnimatedSprite2D` (and a hammer sprite under `HammerPivot`) when there is art, and `Grunt` skips `set_pose` if the method is missing.
  - **Checkpoint**: saves position, `_facing`, `_health`, the state and its `_elapsed`/`_duration`. `load_state()` resumes PAUSE, WALK, CHASE and RECOVER as saved, and **restores a grunt saved in WINDUP or SWING as a fresh RECOVER** (hammer down, harmless), so a restore can never land a surprise hit; the one-hit-per-swing flag and the edge-turn flag are not saved.
- **Tread crawler** (`tread_crawler.gd` + `tread_crawler_art.gd`, `tread_crawler.tscn`; behaviour built, art is **placeholder shapes**): a low tank that trundles along a ledge; its sides hurt, its top beats it. `@tool` `CharacterBody2D` (14×10 rectangle collider, origin at its centre, `collision_layer` 1, `collision_mask` 2 like Drillbot and Grunt) in groups `"enemy"` and `"saveable"`. `facing` and `health` (kicks to destroy it once flipped, default 1) are authored starting values only: runtime state is `_health` and `_facing`. One `enum State`: `WALK` (`speed`, turns at a wall or ledge with the same `test_move()` ledge probe as Grunt, probe shift = body width + 1) → `TURN` (`turn_pause`, still dangerous) → `WALK`; and `FLIPPED` (`flipped_time` 4 s, harmless, solid, standable, the last `WOBBLE_TIME` 1 s the art struggles as the telegraph) → `RIGHTING` (`righting_time`, harmless) → `WALK`. **A player standing on a flipped crawler pins it**: it rights itself only once the top is clear, so it works as a safe stepping stone over spikes or slag.
  - **Flipping**: two child `Area2D`s (layer 0, mask 1), both **polled** each physics frame in `_poll_player()` while it is upright, like Drillbot's drill. `StompZone` is a strip over the top face that overlaps it by 2 px so a player standing on it counts; a player in it with `velocity.y >= 0` (falling or at rest; jumping up through it from the side does nothing) flips the crawler and is given `stomp_bounce` (default 140 px/s, 0 = off) through the existing `launch(vector)` contract, keeping its horizontal speed. `Hazard` is the body footprint grown 2 px at each side and stopping 2 px short of the top; it calls `take_hit(damage, global_position)`. Stomp is tested first, so landing on a corner is a stomp. A kick (`take_damage`) also flips an upright crawler (no damage), so the player can do it from the side when there is no safe top to land on; on a flipped one it costs `health`.
  - **Surf**: `bowled_over()` always true, from either side and in every state; the art is thrown and fades out like the Grunt's.
  - **Checkpoint**: saves position, `_facing`, `_health`, the state, `_elapsed`, `_duration` and the roll; every state resumes as saved, a flipped crawler included.
  - **Placeholder art** (`tread_crawler_art.gd`): shapes drawn in code, facing right and mirrored by `scale.x` = `_facing`; `TreadCrawler` rolls it over with `scale.y` (1 to -1 through 0). Pale plate on the top face (the stomp target, underneath once flipped), dark treads along the bottom (on top once flipped), a lens on the facing side; hull orange while dangerous, grey while stunned. `TreadCrawler` only needs `set_pose(stunned)` to exist on the art node.
  - **Sounds** (planned, in `Audio.PLANNED`): `enemies/crawler_flip`, `enemies/crawler_hit`, `enemies/crawler_bowled`.
- **Proximity mine** (`proximity_mine.gd` + `proximity_mine_art.gd`, `proximity_mine.tscn`; behaviour built, art is **placeholder shapes**): sits on the floor until the player comes near, then arms and goes off. `@tool` `CharacterBody2D` (10×8 rectangle collider, origin at its centre, `collision_layer` 1, `collision_mask` 2 like the other enemies) in groups `"enemy"` and `"saveable"`. Two states: `DORMANT` and `ARMED` (a fuse of `fuse_time` 0.8 s, or the shorter `CHAIN_FUSE` 0.15 s; a fuse can only be shortened, never restarted, and **an armed mine never disarms**, so a player who runs is safe only by being outside `blast_radius` when it goes off). Armed by the player within `trigger_radius` (32) horizontally and `trigger_height` (28) vertically (a dead or dancing player never counts), by `take_damage()` (so a kick or a laser beam arms it and never damages it), or by a neighbour's blast. Every export is a tuning value the script only reads; runtime state is `_state`, `_elapsed` and `_duration`.
  - **Blast**: an immediate `intersect_shape()` circle of `blast_radius` (40) on mask 1 (actors) when the fuse ends or the mine impacts, once per body: the player gets `take_hit(damage, mine position)` (knockback away from the mine), a group-`"enemy"` body gets `take_damage(enemy_damage)` (default 2: a Grunt dies, a Tread crawler is flipped), another mine (found by its `arm()` method) is armed with the chain fuse so mines go off a beat apart rather than recursing. **No line-of-sight test**: it reaches through a thin floor or wall within the radius (a possible refinement: a ground-mask ray, but it must not count a one-way catwalk). It retires first (out of both groups, collision disabled deferred) so nothing it calls can find it, then spawns the standard explosion at `EXPLOSION_SCALE` 1.5 and frees itself.
  - **Kick**: implements `kicked(direction)` as well as `take_damage()`: it slides at `kick_speed` (160 px/s, 0 = a kick only arms it) with `slide_friction` (120 px/s²) while its fuse keeps running. A slide faster than `IMPACT_SPEED` (40) that hits a wall opposing it, or touches a group-`"enemy"` body (the `Contact` `Area2D`, layer 0, mask 1; it also sets off other mines), detonates it at once. So the way to deal with one is to boot it away, ideally into a Grunt. It has no `bowled_over()`: a surfer stops against it, and it will already have armed.
  - **Checkpoint**: saves only its position. A mine saved armed or sliding **restores DORMANT and still**, so a restore can never land a surprise blast; it simply arms again with its whole fuse if the player is near. A mine that has gone off frees itself and is absent from the snapshot.
  - **Placeholder art** (`proximity_mine_art.gd`): a hull with three prongs and a light on top, dim while dormant and blinking red once armed, faster as the fuse runs down; once armed it also draws a ring and faint fill showing the blast radius (the danger zone must be readable, more so on a phone). In the editor it draws the trigger box (cyan) and the blast circle (red), redrawn when either export changes. `ProximityMine` only needs `set_pose(armed, blink_on, fuse)` to exist on the art node.
  - **Sounds** (planned, in `Audio.PLANNED`): `enemies/mine_arm`, `enemies/mine_explode`, `enemies/mine_kick`.
- **Rail bot** (`rail_bot.gd` + `rail_bot_art.gd`, `rail_bot.tscn`, with `spark.gd` + `spark.tscn`; behaviour built, art is **placeholder shapes**): a trolley that rides a `Path2D` rail with a nozzle hanging below it, dripping sparks on a timer. `@tool` `AnimatableBody2D` (12×8 collider at (0, 8), `collision_layer` 1 so the KickHitbox and a mine's blast see it, `collision_mask` 0, `sync_to_physics`, moved by setting `global_position`) in groups `"enemy"` and `"saveable"`. **Placing one**: make it a child of a `Rail` node (see Rail in `platforms-conveyors.md`: drag its markers instead of editing a curve) or of a plain Path2D (it finds its rail itself) or set the `rail` export; its origin is the point on the rail and the body hangs 4–12 px below it, so run the rail just under a ceiling (walls work: it stays upright and the sparks still fall straight down). The first bot to find a rail draws it as an unowned `Line2D` named `RailLine` under the Path2D (added deferred, because a parent Path2D is busy setting up its children, and rebuilt when the curve changes; one line however many bots share the rail). The bot adds its rail to group `\"grab_rail\"` at run time (unless `rail_grabbable` is off) so the player can hang from it, and joins group `\"rail_bot\"` itself, which the player's rail hang treats as an obstacle (see Rail hang in `player.md`). The editor snaps the bot to `start_fraction` and shows a configuration warning with no rail or fewer than two curve points.
  - **Motion**: `speed` (40 px/s) along the baked curve; `mode` `PING_PONG` turns at each end after `end_pause` (0.6 s), `LOOP` wraps (close the curve by repeating its first point as its last, or it jumps); `start_fraction` 0–1.
  - **Sparks**: every `drip_interval` (1.6 s), fully deterministic (no randomness), `phase_offset` staggers several bots like Crusher's. The last `TELEGRAPH` (0.45 s) before a drip shows as a growing glow at the nozzle. A `Spark` (`spark.gd`, a `RigidBody2D` ball: 3 px circle collider, mass 0.1, friction 1, bounce 0.25, `collision_layer` 0, `collision_mask` 2, `can_sleep` off, `gravity_scale` 0) is added beside the bot (or beside the Path2D if the bot is its child) at the nozzle and reset for physics interpolation; it does not inherit the bot's sideways speed. Gravity is added by hand in `_integrate_forces()` at `FALL_ACCEL` 360 px/s² (slower than world gravity so it reads), with vertical speed capped at 170 px/s and total speed at `MAX_SPEED` 220, so the engine only solves contacts. **It is a physical object**: it collides with layer 2 (tiles, platforms, doors, belts, blocks), so it lands, bounces a little and **rolls down slopes**, but it is never solid to actors, the player included. **Rolling resistance** slows its spin from the floor contact: `FLAT_RESISTANCE` (480) on a floor within about 7° of flat, `SLOPE_RESISTANCE` (120) otherwise, so a ball arriving on the flat at 200 px/s stops within about 7 to 9 tiles, a slope under about 7° holds it and 12° and steeper rolls (checked from 12° to 45°). **Conveyors**: a floor contact (normal within about 45° of up) whose body has `get_conveyor_speed()` drags the ball's horizontal speed toward the belt's at `BELT_GRIP` (600 px/s²) and sets its spin to match so it keeps rolling; carried into a wall it holds still against it, carried off the end it falls on. **Despawn**: once it has stayed within `REST_DISTANCE` (2 px) of one spot for `REST_TIME` (0.4 s) it fades out over `FADE_TIME` (0.2 s, 0 = instant), harmless while fading; position decides it rather than velocity, because a resting body's velocity never reads zero (the solver cancels the gravity added each step). `LIFETIME` (15 s) is the backstop for a pit or a loop of belts. **Damage**: a child `Hitbox` (`Area2D`, layer 0, mask 1, 4 px radius) is polled every physics frame; the ball calls `take_hit(spark_damage, position)` on the player and frees itself, whether or not the hit landed. **It is inert towards everything else by design, with one exception, a basin's settled water**: on layer 0 no query sees it (the kick, the bowl sweep, a mine's blast, laser rays, bullets, button and plate sensors, the see-saw's load scan), its mask is layer 2 only so enemies and its own bot are never collided with, it is in no group, and flowing particles and steam ignore it, so sparks can run over any other system without side effects (the reason they are sparks and not slag). A basin's liquid has no collider, so each frame the ball asks `WaterManager.find_basin_at_point()` for the basin under its lowest point and offers that point to `WaterBasin.try_electrify_at()` (see Electrified water in the Liquid system): if the basin accepts, the ball is spent (`queue_free()`, no fade); if it refuses (`conducts_sparks` off, slag, a reaction crust, no liquid over that column, the point still above the surface; a frozen crust over water accepts it, see Frozen crust) the ball falls through the liquid to the floor as it always did. Sparks are not saved and vanish with the level on a respawn.
  - **Power contract**: powered = running. A `PowerTarget` child is in the scene (referenced by uid, so it survives the file moving folders), and `invert` is on the root script like Crusher's. Unpowered it stops where it is, stops dripping and greys out; powered again it carries on. With nothing wired to the PowerTarget it runs forever.
  - **Contact**: a child `Hitbox` (`Area2D`, layer 0, mask 1, 14×10 at (0, 8), a little bigger than the 12×8 body) is polled every physics frame, before the powered check so a stopped bot still hurts, and calls `take_hit(contact_damage, position)` (export, default 1) on anything on layer 1 that has it. The hit knocks the player off a rail hang, a ledge or anything else held (see Rail hang in `player.md`), away from the bot's side; the player's i-frames stop a second hit. The hitbox is switched off when the bot is destroyed.
  - **Damage**: `take_damage(amount)` (the kick / laser / mine-blast contract): costs `health` (default 1, so one hit) and then it explodes with the standard explosion. Out of reach overhead, so a laser or a mine blast is the usual way to take one out.
  - **Checkpoint**: saves its offset along the rail, direction, end pause, health and powered state. **The drip timer is not restored** (it restarts from zero), so a restore can never drop a spark on the respawning player at once.
  - **Placeholder art** (`rail_bot_art.gd`): a trolley wheel, stem, striped body and nozzle; the nozzle glow grows over the telegraph, and it greys out unpowered. `RailBot` only needs `set_pose(charge, powered)` to exist on the art node.
  - **Sounds** (planned, in `Audio.PLANNED`): `enemies/railbot_drip`, `enemies/railbot_hit`.
