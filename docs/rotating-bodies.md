# Rotating bodies, the see-saw and the swing

`RotatingBody` (torque dynamics about a `Fulcrum`), the see-saw scene and the `Swing` that extends it. Read `objon.md` (the core: setup, layers and groups, contracts, invariants, docs index) first.

Covers: Rotating bodies and the see-saw (Riders; Loads; Dynamics; Liquid; Motor and power; Ledge grab; Audio; Checkpoints; Editor and debug; See-saw scene; Mirror surface; Launch; Live pitfalls); Swing / trapeze

- **Rotating bodies and the see-saw** (`res://scenes/interactive/rotating_body.gd`, `class_name RotatingBody`, `@tool`, extends `AnimatableBody2D`; `seesaw.gd` extends it for `seesaw.tscn`): a body that turns about a `Fulcrum` child `Marker2D` under torque dynamics, carrying what stands on it and loaded by it. Layer 2, mask 0; at runtime it joins `"water_solid"`, `"saveable"` and (unless `hangable` is off or `_joins_ground()` is false) `"ground"` (see Ledge grab below), sets `sync_to_physics` and `process_physics_priority` 100 (after riders' own `move_and_slide()`); the dynamics, grip and load scan are runtime-only, and the editor shows the limits and warnings. It rotates its rest transform about the pivot each physics frame (`_apply_angle()`), and `get_angular_velocity()` (rad/s, the real change of angle, so 0 against a stop) is what blocks resting on it read to turn with it. Positive angle, torque and speed are clockwise on screen.
  - **Riders**: engine moving-floor carry plus an **idle grip**: an actor standing still on it (own `|velocity.x|` under `IDLE_SPEED`, on the floor, the body directly beneath) is pinned each frame at its **foot point** (the lowest point of its upright collider) in the body's local space, then snapped onto the face along the face normal (up to `SNAP_TO_FACE`); an actor moving under its own power is released to engine carry and re-anchored once idle. `grip_idle_riders` turns the grip off. Carry alone drifts riders toward the descending end every swing, and anchoring the collider centre slides the feet, hence both parts and the foot point. Rigid blocks are never gripped: they ride by engine contact and their own carry assist.
  - **Loads**: each frame, after moving, `_update_loads()` queries a thin band (`RIDER_BAND`) above the top face of every enabled `CollisionShape2D` child (not `CollisionPolygon2D`, which the load scan, grip, ghosts and art all ignore) and keeps candidates that have weight (`player_weight`, default 2, for the player; a `MeltableBlock`'s `mass`; an enemy's `get_load_weight()` times `enemy_weight_scale`, default 0, so enemies weigh nothing unless a see-saw opts in; see Enemy weight in `enemies.md`) and pass `_is_resting_on()`: an actor is on the floor, not hanging, and a 2px `test_move()` along the face normal hits this body first; a rigid block's own shapes nudged 2px must overlap it (`_rigid_overlaps_support()`). Loads stack: what rests on a block (or, with `enemy_weight_scale` above 0, an enemy) that rests on the body counts at its own x (up to `MAX_STACK_DEPTH`). A load that counted last frame and is still in the band keeps counting for `LOAD_LATCH_FRAMES` after the test starts failing, so a contact glitch doesn't zero the torque. Torque is `weight × (load x − pivot x)`. `get_loads()` entries are `body`, `id`, `weight`, `offset`, `torque`, `position`. `get_net_torque()` is loads plus resting liquid; `get_load_torque()` and `get_liquid_torque()` are its parts.
  - **Dynamics**: torque = loads + liquid + motor − `restoring_torque` × angle (the spring back to level, ignored with `free_rotation` and while the motor runs); angular velocity gains torque / `inertia` × delta, loses `damping` (fraction per second), then `friction_torque / inertia × delta` off its magnitude without crossing zero, so a body at rest under a net torque below `friction_torque` stays put. Without `free_rotation` the angle is clamped to `min_angle_degrees`..`max_angle_degrees` (default ∓25; keep them around 25 or less so riders hold) and velocity into a stop is zeroed, with no bounce; with it the angle wraps to ±π. Loads don't add to inertia (their weights are in torque units where gravity is 1). Defaults (`inertia` 120, `damping` 1.5, `friction_torque` 8, `restoring_torque` 60) are tuned by feel for the 2-tile plank; tune per instance.
  - **Liquid**: each step it reads `WaterManager.get_resting_torque(self)` (the manager runs earlier, so this frame's value), scaled by the per-instance `liquid_weight_scale` (0 = liquid doesn't tip it); the liquid reading is slightly noisy from particles arriving and leaving, which is accepted. See the Liquid system for the carry and weight.
  - **Motor and power**: `motor_torque` (signed) with an angle limit alternates: `_motor_dir` starts at its sign and reverses each time the angle clamps at a stop, so the body oscillates between the limits; the motor must beat any opposing load (a heavy enough rider on the high side holds it against the stop). With `free_rotation` it is a steady drive. It has two subclass hooks: `_get_extra_torque()` (added to loads, liquid and motor each step in `inertia` units, default 0; `Swing` uses it) and `_joins_water_solid()` (default true). It implements the power contract's `set_powered(on)` (powered = the motor runs, unpowered = free; `invert` is exported on the root; `PowerTarget` child in `seesaw.tscn`; unwired the motor is just on, and the default `motor_torque` 0 is a free see-saw).
  - **Ledge grab**: the ends of the body are ledge-hangable like a moving platform's. `hangable` (default true) makes `_ready()` join group `"ground"`, which is all `_is_hangable_surface()` reads for a non-tile body; `is_hangable()` is the duck-typed say the player asks at the grab and every frame of the hang and mantle (the contract `MeltableBlock` also implements): true only while the tilt from the rest pose (wrapped, so a free-rotation body counts too) is within `hang_max_tilt_degrees` (default 15) and `|get_angular_velocity()|` is within `hang_max_angular_speed` (default 0.8 rad/s; 0 against a stop). Past either the grab is refused and a held hang or mantle is let go through `_force_release_hang()` with the usual regrab lockout; within them `_follow_hang_platform()` carries the pinned hang and mantle points through the body's full transform change, so a hanging rider swings with the tilt. The hook `_joins_ground()` (default true; false in `Swing`, whose `is_hangable()` is therefore false and which never joins the group) is the subclass's say, like `_joins_water_solid()`. Exports are in the "Hang" group. Only the ends hold a grab: the top-edge probes (forward clearance, headroom, ledge-top ray) find an 8 px plank end across the whole ±25° range (modelled offline against the player's probe constants: a window of about 6 to 13 px of hand height, 7 px level, more than one frame of fall at 300 px/s); a plank's underside is never grabbable (the catwalk underside hang reads group `"catwalk"` only). A hanging player is not a load (`_is_resting_on()` excludes it), so hanging from one end doesn't tip the body; only standing on it does.
  - **Audio**: `creak_sound` (a looping `Audio.make_loop()` child at the Fulcrum, so it doesn't swing; its level eases toward `|angular velocity| / creak_full_speed` over `CREAK_FADE`, volume and pitch follow it, and the loop stops once faded out so a still see-saw costs nothing) and `stop_sound` (a positional one-shot when the angle clamps, from `STOP_QUIET_DB` at `stop_min_speed` to 0 dB at `stop_full_speed`, nothing below it); empty ids disable either. Ids `interactive/seesaw_creak` and `interactive/seesaw_thud` are in `Audio.PLANNED` (no files authored yet). A checkpoint restore plays neither.
  - **Checkpoints**: `save_state()` is `angle`, `angular_velocity` (the integrated one) and `motor_dir`; `load_state()` clamps the angle, snaps, clears the grip anchors, loads, latches and torques, calls `WaterManager.forget_motion(self)`, and teleports the physics body: `_teleport_physics_body()` switches the server body to static and straight back to kinematic before setting its transform, so the server takes it directly instead of deriving a one-frame velocity spike that flings blocks off the plank, and `_reset_interpolation_tree()` resets physics interpolation on the body and every child (it only clears one node at a time). That relies on how Godot's physics server treats a body's first transform after becoming kinematic; re-check it after engine upgrades. The plank still shows at its flat pose for one rendered frame after a restore; accepted.
  - **Editor and debug**: translucent ghosts of every collision shape at `min_angle_degrees` (blue) and `max_angle_degrees` (orange) while editing (`show_limits_in_editor`, skipped with `free_rotation`), and configuration warnings for a missing `Fulcrum` or collision shape or inverted limits. `debug_draw` (flipped at runtime by `debug_toggle_key`) adds a top-level overlay drawn in world space: the pivot, each load's lever arm with weight and torque (orange clockwise, blue counter-clockwise), the net torque as an arc (half a turn at `debug_full_arc_torque`), the angle, and `liquid` and `motor` lines when non-zero; `debug_loads` prints a line whenever a body in the band fails the resting test.
  - **See-saw scene** (`seesaw.tscn`, root script `seesaw.gd`, `@tool`): a 32×8 plank `CollisionShape2D` (2 tiles, one either side of the `Fulcrum` at its centre), the placeholder `PlankArt` (`seesaw_plank_art.gd`: wood and a 1px outline drawn from the parent's collision shapes, so it follows shape edits; replace it with sprites), and a `PowerTarget`. Per-instance exports in a "Plank" group, so a level needn't turn on Editable Children: `length_tiles` (default 2, step 0.5, 16px tiles), `plank_texture` (tiled across the length by an unsaved `PlankSkin` `Sprite2D` with `region_rect` and texture repeat, so not an `AtlasTexture`; hides the placeholder art; its height is the texture's and `texture_offset` shifts it; lengths that aren't a whole number of texture widths end in a cut tile), `show_pivot` / `pivot_radius` / `pivot_color` (an unsaved `PivotMarker` child kept last so it draws over the art and the skin) and `mirror_surface` (default off; see the mirror surface bullet below). `_apply_plank()` (setters and `_ready()`, before `super()`) gives the node named `CollisionShape2D` its own new `RectangleShape2D` of `length_tiles × 16` by the scene shape's thickness, re-applied on every load because changes to a non-editable child aren't saved with the level, so that node's width in the scene is overridden. For another shape (a T), turn on Editable Children or make an inherited scene and add more `CollisionShape2D` children (anything not named `CollisionShape2D` is left alone), moving the `Fulcrum` to the intersection; the skin covers only the original plank, so skin the rest with sprites. A body with more than one shape is untested. Stands are built from tilemap sprites without physics.
  - **Mirror surface** (`mirror_surface`, the see-saw only): the plank's whole top face reflects lasers. `_apply_mirror_surface()` (setter and `_ready()`, runtime only) joins group `"laser_mirror"` when the export is on and the node named `CollisionShape2D` holds a rectangle, and leaves it otherwise. `get_laser_segment()` is the shape's top edge (local −Y) taken through `get_pose_global()` and moved 1 px (`MIRROR_LIFT`) along the face normal, and `get_laser_normal()` is local up through the same pose; `RotatingBody.get_pose_global()` builds the pose from `_angle`, never the node transform, which lags a write under `sync_to_physics`. The plank stays a real solid (`laser_body_is_physical()` is true), so a beam onto its underside or an end stops there and only the front reflects; no kick or bullet is involved, so the plank turns only by what tips it. The turret solves before the see-saw moves (`process_physics_priority` 100), so the reflected beam trails the plank by one frame, accepted for slow planks; a receiver on such a beam needs its `hold_time`. The placeholder art draws a bright line along the top face while the export is on. What rests on the plank is treated as anywhere else, which makes the plank usable as a puzzle piece: a block on it shields the face from a beam coming from above (it is opaque, and the collider overlaps the 1 px lift so the beam reaches the block first), a block that is lit melts onto it, the player standing on it is burned, and liquid is invisible to beams, so a pour of water onto the plank tips it (`liquid_weight_scale`) and steers the reflected beam without ever shielding it. Liquid is the natural aiming load.
  - **Launch** (`seesaw.gd` only, opt-in; `launch_gain` 0 = off, so an existing plank is unchanged): when the plank clamps at a stop, `RotatingBody` calls the hook `_stop_reached(speed, side)` (`speed` the angular speed it arrived with, `side` +1 at the clockwise stop, -1 at the counter-clockwise one; never with `free_rotation`), and the see-saw's override throws what rests on the **raised** end. It does nothing below `launch_min_speed` (rad/s, default 0.2, the thud's own threshold), with `launch_gain` 0, or while the motor runs (`get_motor_torque() != 0`) unless `launch_when_motorised` (default off: a motorised plank hits a stop every cycle). Otherwise each entry of last frame's `get_loads()` whose body has `launch()` and sits on the raised side of the pivot (its offset along the plank axis, opposite to `side`) gets `launch(normal * min(speed * distance from the pivot * launch_gain, launch_max_speed))` (default cap 500 px/s), where `normal` is the plank's face normal at the stop (`get_laser_normal()`, so 25° from straight up at a full stop) and the distance is the load's own distance from the pivot, so standing at the tip throws furthest. The face normal leans toward the lowered end, so a thrown rider is flung up and back over the pivot, toward the side that came down, not away from the plank. Loads on the lowered end, which is where whatever tipped the plank stands, are never thrown; enemies have no `launch()` and are skipped. The existing contract does the rest (the player buffers it and drops held states; a block divides it by `mass ^ launch_weight_exponent`, so a metal block barely moves at gains that fling the player). Each launched body's anchor is erased from the grip and it is stamped in `_launched_frame`; for `LOAD_LATCH_FRAMES` after that `_launched_recently()` keeps the grip from pinning it back and the load latch from counting it, so it is neither dragged onto the face nor thrown twice. A stop reached at under 1 rad/s, as a block dropped on a 2-tile plank does (about 0.3), gives only about 10 px/s per 32 px of lever at gain 1, so useful gains are in the tens. Nothing is saved (`load_state()` clears the stamps). Inspector group "Launch": `launch_gain`, `launch_min_speed`, `launch_max_speed`, `launch_when_motorised`, `launch_sound` (`interactive/seesaw_launch`, in `Audio.PLANNED`, no file yet; played once per stop at the first body thrown; a restore plays nothing).
  - **Live pitfalls**: use an overlap query, not `test_move()`, for a rigid block's resting test (its mask includes layer 1, so the probe can report a player standing on it as the support's replacement and fail for as long as they stay); never dereference a stored load's `body` across frames (a melted block frees itself): use its `id`; the restore teleport and interpolation reset above must stay together; everything on the plank is read from the transform after `_apply_angle()`, so keep the scan after it; anything read earlier in the frame (the laser solver) takes the pose from `get_pose_global()`, not the node.
- **Swing / trapeze** (`swing.gd`, `class_name Swing`, `@tool`, extends `RotatingBody`; `swing.tscn`, placeholder art `swing_art.gd`): a rigid rod from the `Fulcrum` to the `Grip` (the bar), authored hanging straight down. **Length**: `length_tiles` (16px tiles, step 0.5, default 4 = 64px) places the Grip that far from the Fulcrum along its authored direction (straight down if it sits on the Fulcrum), on every load as well as on change, since edits to a non-editable child aren't saved with the level; edit the export, not the Grip's position. The editor arc, grab circle and the pendulum period (`2π√(L/g)`) follow it; `push_accel` and `max_pump_speed` are angular, so a longer rod is faster at the bar for the same settings. No collision shapes, layers 0/0, not in `"water_solid"` (`_joins_water_solid()` is false) or `"ground"` (`_joins_ground()` is false), so the bar is not solid, nothing rests on it and it is never ledge-grabbed (the player's swing grab is its own); the base supplies the rotation about the Fulcrum, damping, friction, angle limits (±80° in the scene), motor/power, the creak/thud audio and checkpoint save/restore (angle, angular velocity). **Pendulum**: `_get_extra_torque()` returns `inertia * gravity * r.x / |r|²` (r = grip − pivot, i.e. `-(g/L) sin θ`, valid for any authored pose; period `2π√(L/g)`, 1.68 s for the 64px default rod at `gravity` 900) plus the push; `restoring_torque` is 0 in `swing.tscn` (the base's linear spring would fight the pendulum), with `damping` 0.2 and `friction_torque` 1.5. **Push**: the rider's left/right arrives through `set_push(dir)`; it is a constant tangential `push_accel` (3 rad/s²) toward that side, tapering to nothing as the angular speed along the motion approaches `max_pump_speed` (4 rad/s), so in-phase pumping peaks near 66° on a 64px rod instead of hitting the limits; pushing against the motion brakes at full strength. **Rider contract** (duck-typed with player.gd): `grab_radius` (10 px), `get_grip_global()`, `attach_rider()`/`detach_rider()`, `catch_momentum(velocity)` (the player's velocity as it grabs the bar becomes angular velocity, a point mass on a rigid rod: `ω = (r × v) / |r|²`, the radial part absorbed by the rod, blended with the swing's own ω by `catch_transfer`, 1 = take the player's outright, 0 = keep the swing's own), `set_push()`, `get_velocity_at(world_point)` (`ω × r` from the last step's angular velocity, 0 against a stop); after moving, the swing pins the rider's hand to the grip itself, and drops the rider if it stops hanging or its `hang_swing` isn't this swing. A rod's angle limits are reached only by a launch or a very strong rider; there is no slack and no rope. A hanging player is not physics-resolved (like every hang), so the arc between the limits must be clear of solid tiles. Sounds `interactive/swing_creak` and `interactive/swing_thud` are in `Audio.PLANNED`; grabbing plays `player/hang`, jumping off `player/jump`. The editor draws the arc between the limits and the grab radius (`SwingArt`, editor only). Place the swing before the Player in the tree so the player draws in front. **Live pitfall**: `sync_to_physics` makes the body's node transform (and its children's, the `Grip` too) lag a write until the end of the physics step, so inside a step the pose comes from `_angle` (`get_grip_global()`), never from the node, or the rider's hand trails the bar by a tick. `RotatingBody`'s own grip and load scan read the node after `_apply_angle()` and so see the previous step's pose; not checked whether that matters for the see-saw.
